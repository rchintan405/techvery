Privacy Policy

Understand how we collect, protect, and handle your data.

Effective date: 19 August 2026 | Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. Introduction

Techvery (“Techvery,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit https://techvery.com, submit an enquiry through our website, request information about our services, subscribe to our communications, or otherwise contact us.

By using our website or submitting information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you complete our contact form or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Company name
  • The contents of your message
  • Information about the services or solutions you are interested in
  • Project requirements or quotation details
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

If you subscribe to our marketing communications, we may also collect your email address and communication preferences.

Please do not submit sensitive, confidential, financial, identification, or unnecessary personal information through the website’s general contact form.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access our website, limited technical information may be processed automatically to operate, deliver, and secure the website. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages or resources requested
  • Technical and security logs

We do not use this information to create behavioural advertising profiles.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Receive and respond to your enquiries
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Understand your business or project requirements
  • Prepare quotations, proposals, or service recommendations
  • Take steps requested by you before entering into a business relationship
  • Maintain records of enquiries and business communications
  • Send marketing communications where you have provided your consent
  • Manage subscriptions and unsubscribe requests
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure our website
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents
  • Improve our website, services, and communications
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal rights

We will not use your personal information for purposes that are materially different from those described above without providing appropriate notice or obtaining consent where required.

4. Basis for Processing

Where required under applicable law, we process personal information on one or more of the following grounds:

  • You have provided your consent
  • Processing is necessary to respond to your request or take steps before entering into an agreement
  • Processing is necessary to perform an agreement with you
  • Processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation
  • Processing is necessary to protect our legitimate business, legal, operational, or security interests, where permitted by law

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at info@techvery.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Marketing Communications

We will send promotional or marketing communications only where you have requested them or otherwise provided appropriate consent.

You may unsubscribe at any time by:

  • Selecting the “Unsubscribe” link included in a marketing email
  • Emailing info@techvery.com with your unsubscribe request

After you unsubscribe, we may retain limited information necessary to record and respect your preference.

Unsubscribing from marketing communications will not prevent us from sending necessary communications concerning an enquiry, quotation, project, agreement, or existing business relationship.

6. Cookies

Techvery.com currently uses only cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the website’s operation, security, and essential functionality.

Essential cookies may be used to:

  • Enable core website functions
  • Maintain website security
  • Prevent fraudulent or malicious activity
  • Manage website traffic
  • Remember information required during a browsing session
  • Maintain website stability and performance

Because essential cookies are necessary for the website to function, they generally cannot be disabled through the website. You may block them through your browser settings, but doing so could cause parts of the website to work incorrectly.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural-tracking cookies, social-media tracking pixels, or non-essential analytics cookies.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and obtain consent where required.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information only where reasonably necessary with:

  • Website hosting, email, IT, and cybersecurity service providers
  • Contractors or service providers supporting our business operations
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants, auditors, or insurers
  • Business partners involved in preparing or delivering a service you have requested, subject to appropriate confidentiality requirements
  • Government authorities, regulators, courts, or law-enforcement agencies where disclosure is required by law
  • A successor or relevant party in connection with a proposed or completed restructuring, investment, merger, acquisition, or transfer of our business

Service providers are permitted to process information only for authorised purposes and are expected to protect it appropriately.

8. International Data Transfers

Our website, email, hosting, or other technical service providers may process or store information outside the United Arab Emirates.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take reasonable and legally required measures to ensure that it receives an appropriate level of protection. These measures may include contractual safeguards or another legally permitted transfer mechanism.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

The applicable retention period may depend on:

  • The nature of your enquiry
  • Whether your enquiry results in a quotation, project, or business relationship
  • The duration of our communications with you
  • Applicable contractual, accounting, legal, or regulatory requirements
  • The need to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we will delete, anonymise, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Information Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access or disclosure
  • Accidental loss
  • Misuse
  • Alteration
  • Destruction
  • Other unlawful processing

However, no internet transmission, website, email service, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, identification documents, banking information, or other sensitive information through our general contact form.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations or exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • Ask how your personal information is being used
  • Request access to or a copy of your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request restriction or suspension of certain processing
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Request the transfer of your information where applicable
  • Object to decisions based solely on automated processing where applicable

Techvery.com does not currently use solely automated decision-making to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

To submit a privacy request, email info@techvery.com. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before completing the request.

12. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. They are not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact info@techvery.com so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

13. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

We do not control and are not responsible for the content, privacy practices, security, or availability of third-party websites. You should review their respective privacy policies before submitting personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, services, business practices, or legal obligations.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Where appropriate, we may provide additional notice of material changes.
